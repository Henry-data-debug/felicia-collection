Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your handbags and fashion items locally made or imported?

All our products are 100% directly imported from renowned international fashion and design hubs in Italy, the UK, Turkey, and top global luxury manufacturing centers. Our focus is exclusively on curating the finest imported fashion and home essentials.

How does delivery work in Nairobi and across Kenya?

Within Nairobi, we offer convenient same-day or next-day rider delivery directly to your home or office. For deliveries outside Nairobi (Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Thika, etc.), we ship via trusted couriers (Fargo Courier, Wells Fargo, Speedaf) with delivery within 24 to 48 hours.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa (Buy Goods / Till & Paybill), direct bank transfers, and cash-on-delivery for verified Nairobi deliveries.

How can I order?

Ordering is fast and effortless! Simply click the "Order via WhatsApp" button on any product card or text us directly at +254 700 000 000. Our concierge will confirm availability, size/color preferences, and dispatch your order promptly.
